# Content wave 1: native-speaker review

Worlds 3–5 (59 new levels). Please check every row; anything that sounds unnatural, has a wrong split or a wrong stress, write the fix next to it and send it back.

- **Split**: how a Ukrainian primer would divide the word into syllables (one vowel each).
- **Stress**: the stressed vowel carries ́ (e.g. СО-ВА́). One-syllable words have none.
- **Sentences**: should sound like something a parent would say; case forms, agreement and у/в correct.

## Words I was unsure about

- **ШАЛИК**: standard (СУМ) but less common than ШАРФ; ШАРФ needs Ф (World 7).
- **ОЛЕКСАНДР** split О-ЛЕК-САНДР: the last syllable is a heavy closed one; is that how you'd clap it?
- Closed-syllable splits: ДЕРЖ-ПРОМ, ХУСТ-КА, ЯБ-ЛУ-КО, КАР-ТОП-ЛЯ, ВИШ-НЯ, ВІД-РО, МІТ-ЛА.
- **СОЙКА**, **ТАЙМЕР**, **КОВБОЙ**: few everyday Й words have a clear picture; swap any that feel odd.
- «Дід пасе козу.» and «Сова несе перо.»: the picture shows them *near* / *holding*; is the verb fine for a 5-year-old?
- «Мій робот.» and «Я Мишко!»: first-person sentences; the picture shows just the robot / him.
- у/в: «Мама в автобусі», «Ложка в каші», «Качка в човні» (after a vowel) vs «Бобер у воді», «Олень у лісі» (after a consonant or before в).
